Mohammed Kudus — alias Bazaki, childhood nickname "Kuku" — is one of the most explosive right wingers in world football today. From the dirt pitches of Nima, Accra, he rose through the Right to Dream Academy, dominated the Danish Superliga, won back-to-back Eredivisie titles at Ajax, electrified the Premier League at West Ham, and then completed a £55 million move to Tottenham Hotspur in July 2025 — signing until 2031.

Spurs Debut · vs Burnley · Aug 16, 2025

In his first appearance in Lilywhite, Kudus delivered two assists in a clinical 3-0 Premier League win over Burnley. Thomas Frank said he has "a lot more to show." The N17 crowd gave him a standing ovation. It was, as Kuku himself said — "just the beginning."

FIFA Puskás Award Nominee

His solo goal vs SC Freiburg won West Ham's Goal of the Season and earned a 2024 FIFA Puskás Award nomination — the sport's most prestigious individual goal recognition.

Ghana · International Honours

2× Ghana Footballer of the Year. Scored on his international debut. Two goals at the 2022 FIFA World Cup in Qatar. The undisputed star of the Black Stars generation.

Premier League & Champions League · London, England · Shirt #20

In the summer of 2025, Tottenham boss Thomas Frank made Kudus his marquee signing — £55 million from West Ham United. Wearing #20, Mo lit up his Premier League debut against Burnley with two assists in a commanding 3-0 victory. His direct, fearless wing play and technical brilliance has made him an instant fan favourite at N17. Now competing in the UEFA Champions League, the 25-year-old Ghanaian is playing on the biggest stage of his career.
