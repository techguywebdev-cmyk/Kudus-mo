The Man · The Myth

Born In
Nima. Built For The World.

Mohammed Kudus — alias Bazaki, childhood nickname "Kuku" — is one of the most explosive right wingers in world football. From the dirt pitches of Nima, Accra, he rose through the Right to Dream Academy, dominated the Danish Superliga, won back-to-back Eredivisie titles at Ajax, electrified the Premier League at West Ham, then completed a £55M move to Tottenham Hotspur in July 2025 — signing until 2031.
